As fraud tactics continue to evolve, financial institutions face growing pressure to streamline operations, improve customer experience, and responsibly apply automation and AI . Despite this pressure, human judgment remains fundamental to striking the right balance: stopping bad actors and identifying fraud trends without blocking legitimate customers or creating unnecessary friction during onboarding and account servicing. The human in the loop remains one of the most powerful tools risk teams have, and that responsibility carries real pressure.
