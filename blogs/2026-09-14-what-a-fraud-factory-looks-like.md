The storefront on Burbank Boulevard looked forgettable; that was probably the point. Next door to an auto body shop, with blinds half-drawn across a window advertising shipping, receiving, and a scan-to-PDF service, it offered passersby only a partial glimpse inside: rows of small mailboxes and a clerk at the desk sorting mail.
